Bows MiniNet Plugin

Bow Domain services require the Bows MiniNet plugin. With the plugin enabled, the browser address bar can be used for supported MiniNet domains.

Domains purchased or rented through Bows MiniNet are only accessible in browsers where the MiniNet plugin is installed and enabled.

Domain Rentals & Purchases

MiniNet domain rentals and one-time purchases are planned, but they are currently paused while the system is still being developed.

Once available, users may be able to rent temporary domains, purchase permanent domains, or use trial domains depending on the options offered at that time.

Checking Domain Availability

If you want to check whether a MiniNet domain is available, you can do so through the Bows MiniNet plugin dashboard.

The dashboard can check official, rented, permanent, and temporary MiniNet domain records.

Important Notice

Bow Domains are not normal public internet domains. They are custom MiniNet addresses designed to work through the Bow MiniNet system.

This site can open self-hosted MiniNet pages, but full Bow Domain routing through the browser address bar requires the Bows MiniNet plugin.
