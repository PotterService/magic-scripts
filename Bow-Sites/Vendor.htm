How MiniNet Domains Work

MiniNet domains are not normal public internet domains. They are custom browser-based domains that work through the Bows MiniNet plugin.

When a supported MiniNet domain is entered into the browser, the plugin checks the MiniNet domain database and routes the domain to its assigned forwarding URL or Bow-hosted page.

Example:

unicorns.rocks
routes to a MiniNet page or forwarding URL configured in the MiniNet database.

Important Notice

MiniNet domains purchased or rented through Bow MiniNet are only accessible when the Bows MiniNet plugin is installed and enabled in the browser.

MiniNet domains are not ICANN public domains and do not replace normal public internet domain names.

Some domains may be reserved for public use, system use, security reasons, or future MiniNet services.

Availability Checks

Domain availability can be checked through the Bows MiniNet plugin dashboard. The dashboard can check official, rented, permanent, temporary, and reserved MiniNet domain records.

If a domain is already taken, expired, reserved, or unavailable, the dashboard will show the domain status.
