YouTube Watch Time Tracker Pro Plus

A custom Bow script that tracks your YouTube watch time, watched videos, top channels, and local leaderboard directly in your browser.

Fully open source and community editable. Built by Bow Service for creators, developers, and YouTube users.

What It Does

Tracks how long you watch YouTube videos and saves stats by channel. It can show total watch time, videos watched, and your top channels.

Local Stats

Your stats are saved locally in your browser using localStorage. No account, cloud upload, or outside database is required.

Leaderboard

The script includes a leaderboard so you can see which YouTube channels you watch the most.

Privacy Notice

This tracker is local-only. Your YouTube watch stats are stored directly inside your browser using localStorage.

Bow Service does not upload, collect, sell, transmit, or store your viewing data on external servers.

Exported backup files are fully controlled by the user.

How To Use

1

Install and enable the YouTube tracker script in Magic Scripts.

2

Open YouTube and watch videos normally.

3

Click the tracker or View Stats button to open your leaderboard.

4

Use Export Data if you want to back up your stats.

Saved Data Keys

The script stores data under these local browser keys:

ytChannelStatsSafe
ytVideoWatchStatsSafe

Channel Tracking

Saves watch time by channel, channel videos watched, channel URL, and channel logo when available.

Video Tracking

Saves video title, channel name, total seconds watched, and how many times the video was watched.

Export / Import

The built-in settings panel lets you export your tracker data as JSON and import it again later.

Important

If you clear browser data, reset the script, uninstall the script, or use another browser profile, your saved tracker stats may be removed.

Export your data if you want a backup.
