Ownership Request

Request Ownership Transfer

Important: Bow Card Vault may reach out to the previous owner to verify transfer of ownership. If confirmation is not received, the request may stay pending or be rejected.

Tip: To make transfers quicker, it may be faster if the previous owner submits the transfer request from their own email.

How to submit by email

  1. Fill out the form below.
  2. Click Create Email Request.
  3. Before sending the email, attach proof such as a receipt, letter of sale, transfer message, or purchase confirmation.
  4. Also attach a clear photo of the card/slab/item.

Note: This static website cannot automatically attach files to your email. Please attach them manually before sending.
